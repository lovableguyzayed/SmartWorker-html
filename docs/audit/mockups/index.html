Batch 1 — Sign in and sign up · approved and built

AUTH-01, AUTH-03, AUTH-09, COMP-01. Today: two separate screens. Sign-up has 9 fields, mandatory email, and Send/Verify buttons side by side. After: one screen for everyone. Enter the mobile number (+91 already there), get the code, and a first-timer adds only their name. Password sign-in stays one tap away for staff accounts. Terms, Privacy and WhatsApp help are linked. Depends on D1: OTP sign-in needs an SMS provider.

Batch 2 — Bottom tabs, large text, dates · approved and built

NAV-01, A11Y-01, DATE-01. Tabs: 6 tabs at 35px each became 5 at about 70px. Profile leaves the bar, since it is already the round button at the top right of Home. Scan moves to the centre, where the thumb rests. Attendance header: the subtitle goes, and the date picker, arrows and long written-out date become one row: ‹ Tue, 10 Nov 2026 ›. Search and site share a row, so workers show on the first screen even at the largest text. Dates: "Tuesday, November 10, 2026" (US order) becomes "Tue, 10 Nov 2026" / "मंगल, 10 नवंबर 2026" everywhere.
