Every screen, before and after

All 30 screens of the app on a phone: the original on the left, what is live now on the right

11 November 2026 · after pull requests 23 and 24 · same test data on both sides

How to read it

  • Before is the app as it was when the audit started.
  • After is the app now, with the audit's fixes and the other session's features (PF/ESI, minimum wage, backup, SMS).
  • Each picture shows the top of the screen at a 390px-wide phone. Long screens are cut at about two and a half phone-heights.
  • The names and numbers are test data, not your real workers.

Changes you will see on almost every screen

  • Bottom bar: five tabs with Scan in the centre, instead of six.
  • Dates the Indian way (Wed, 11 Nov 2026).
  • Nothing runs off the side at small phones or the largest text size.

The PDFs

Every PDF the app makes is in the pdfs folder beside this file: payslip (Save PDF and Print), ID card and worker report, each in English and Hindi.
